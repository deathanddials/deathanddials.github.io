About This Watch

This Hamilton Thin-o-matic represents exactly what the brand was doing well in the mid-1960s — a slim automatic movement in a clean dress case designed for American tastes. The Model H reference sits in an accessible sweet spot: interesting enough for collectors but priced for daily wear.

It was selected for the qualities that hold up over time: strong original dial, an unpolished case that hasn't been cleaned of its history, and a movement that runs without complaint. These are the details that matter when you're deciding whether a watch is worth living with.
