Sell or Trade

Your watch deserves a straight offer.

No lowball games. No week-long silence. You'll hear back within 24–48 hours with a real number or an honest explanation of why it's not a fit right now.

What We Buy

American and European vintage from roughly 1930–1980. Mechanical watches in working condition or clearly original examples worth preserving. The kinds of pieces that tell a story.

Hamilton Bulova Elgin Benrus Longines Omega Enicar Universal Genève Girard-Perregaux Waltham Illinois Gruen

Strong candidates

  • All-original dials — no reprints, no restored lume
  • Unpolished cases with honest wear
  • Interesting or unusual dials — sector, two-tone, early digital
  • Working mechanical movements
  • Military or field watches from US or European makers
  • Complete sets with original box or papers (adds value)

Likely not a fit

  • Battery-powered quartz fashion watches
  • Heavily polished or case-refinished examples
  • Watches with replacement dials or heavily restored parts
  • Broken movements with no clear path to service
  • Reproductions or unverifiable pieces
